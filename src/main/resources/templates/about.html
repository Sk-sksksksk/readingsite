About

Readingsite is a small, quiet place for reading. It holds short stories, journal pages, ghazals and quotes, written in Hindi and in English.

Two languages, kept apart

Each piece is written in one language and stays that way. Nothing here is a translation of something else. If you read Hindi, you can choose हिन्दी; if you read English, you can choose English; and if you read both, everything is here for you.

Finding something to read

You can browse by language, type or mood, search for a word, or start from how you are feeling today. The collections are small shelves gathered by hand.

What you will not find

There are no comments, no likes, no audio and no accounts for readers. The site is meant to be read slowly and left quietly.

Reading comfortably

On every piece you can make the text larger or smaller, and the site works with a keyboard and with screen readers. If something gets in your way, I would like to know.
